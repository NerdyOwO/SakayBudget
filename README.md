# SakayBudget – Website

**A Budget-First Commuting Fare Estimation and Planning System for Filipino Commuters**

SakayBudget is a web-based commuting fare estimation and budget planning system developed to help Filipino commuters calculate transportation costs and organize their daily or weekly commuting expenses. The website provides users with an accessible platform for estimating fares, planning journeys, and monitoring their transportation budget. It is designed to assist students, employees, and individuals who want to manage their commuting allowances more effectively.

The website features a fare calculator that allows users to enter their origin and destination, select their preferred mode of transportation, and provide passenger details to generate an estimated fare. It supports different public transportation options, including traditional and modern jeepneys, ordinary and air-conditioned buses, and traditional and modern UV Express vehicles. Its fare calculations are based on official fare references published by the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB).

SakayBudget also allows users to combine multiple rides into one trip and calculate the estimated cost of a round-trip journey. This helps commuters account for transportation expenses when their journeys involve transfers or returning to their starting point. In addition, the website includes a budget planner that projects commuting expenses based on the number of commuting days and the user's available allowance.

To make expense monitoring easier, the website provides a dashboard that summarizes the user's budget, estimated spending, and trip activity. It also includes a trip history feature that allows users to review previously recorded journeys, check their expenses, and reuse or manage saved trip information.

Through its combination of fare calculation and budget management features, SakayBudget aims to provide a practical solution to the challenge of planning transportation expenses. Rather than focusing only on finding routes, the website places greater emphasis on helping commuters understand how much they may need to spend and whether their available budget can cover their planned journeys.

**Key Features:**
- **Fare Calculator:** Estimates fares based on the selected origin, destination, transportation type, and passenger details.
- **Multiple Transportation Options:** Supports several common public transportation modes in the Philippines.
- **Multiple Ride Entries:** Allows users to combine different rides and transfers into one trip.
- **Round-Trip Calculation:** Calculates estimated expenses for both the outgoing and return journeys.
- **Budget Planner:** Projects commuting expenses across one to seven commuting days.
- **Dashboard:** Provides an overview of the user's allowance, spending, and commuting activity.
- **Trip History:** Saves and organizes recorded trips for future reference.
- **Customizable Interface:** Includes settings for appearance, text size, and other user preferences.
- **Local Data Storage:** Uses browser storage to retain saved trip information and user settings on the device.
