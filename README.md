# SakayBudget

**A Budget-First Commuting Fare Estimation and Planning System for Filipino Commuters**

SakayBudget is an Android application designed to help Filipino commuters estimate their transportation expenses and manage their daily commuting budgets. It provides a convenient way to calculate fares, plan trips, and monitor transportation spending through a mobile device. The application aims to help students, employees, and other commuters make more informed decisions about their daily travel expenses.

The application features a fare calculator that allows users to select their starting point, destination, transportation type, and passenger details to estimate their commuting costs. Its fare calculations are based on official fare references published by the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB). Users can also add multiple rides to a single trip and calculate round-trip expenses to get a better estimate of their total transportation costs.

Aside from fare estimation, SakayBudget includes a budget planner that allows users to project their commuting expenses over several days. Its dashboard provides an overview of their budget, estimated spending, and remaining allowance. The application also includes a trip history feature where users can review previously recorded trips and their expenses.

By bringing fare estimation and budget planning into one mobile application, SakayBudget aims to make commuting expense management more accessible and convenient. It serves as a practical budgeting tool that helps users prepare for their transportation expenses and manage their allowances more responsibly.

**Key Features:**
- **Fare Calculator:** Estimates transportation fares based on the selected route, transportation type, and passenger details.
- **Multiple Ride Entries:** Allows users to include several rides in one trip.
- **Round-Trip Calculation:** Estimates the total cost of traveling to a destination and returning.
- **Budget Planner:** Helps users project their commuting expenses for up to seven days.
- **Dashboard:** Displays budget information, spending summaries, and commuting activity.
- **Trip History:** Stores and displays previously recorded trips for easy reference.
- **Customizable Settings:** Provides options for adjusting the application's appearance and preferences.
